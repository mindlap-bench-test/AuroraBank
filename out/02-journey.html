1. FD Compare-and-Book HERO

As a Prospective Customer, I want to compare fixed-deposit tenures and book one in a single conversation, so that I can put idle money to work without hunting across separate pages and forms.

  • Given I mention an amount and that I want it parked safely, when I ask the assistant, then it compares tenures (e.g. the 15-month special vs. the standard 1-year) and recommends one.
  • Given I want to book the recommended FD, when I confirm, then the assistant tells me booking moves money and requires login plus an OTP step-up.
  • Given I've logged in and completed the OTP step-up, when I confirm again, then the FD is booked and I see confirmation in the conversation.

Today

  1. No single search across the site; different sections for rates, calculator, and booking. assume

  2. Separate page from the rates table just viewed. assume

  3. Re-enters the deposit amount for each tenure tried. assume

  4. "That's — what, four, five different sections?" — Rohan Desai.

  5. Amount and tenure typed again in a new form. assume

  6. Final step-up + submit, same as the assistant path but after five extra steps. assume

With the assistant

  1. "I've got 5 lakh sitting idle" → tenure comparison appears inline, no separate calculator.

  2. E.g. the 15-month special over the 1-year, for the extra yield.

  3. No separate booking form; the same conversation carries the deposit details through.

  4. Booking lands in Corvus assume: direct booking vs. handoff to existing form — open.

2. Card Recommend-and-Apply HERO

As a Prospective or Mid-Journey Customer, I want the assistant to recommend a card based on how I spend and pick up an application I abandoned, so that I don't have to restart a long form or explain myself twice.

  • Given I mention a reason (e.g. a trip), when I answer a couple of spend questions, then the assistant recommends a card (Aurora Voyage, Everyday, or Infinia) with a reason.
  • Given I want to apply, when I confirm, then the assistant walks me through the application a few questions at a time instead of a long form.
  • Given I'm a returning customer who started an application before, when I log in, then the assistant recognizes the abandoned application and offers to resume it.

Today

  1. No guided recommendation based on how the person actually spends. assume

  2. "Our credit card application completion rate is embarrassing." — Rohan Desai.

  3. "People start, they hit a page asking for something they don't have handy, they leave." — Rohan Desai.

  4. ~60% of people who start a card application don't finish it — Meena Iyer.

  5. Adds to call-center volume for "where's my card application" queries.

With the assistant

  1. A couple of questions, then a specific recommendation with reasoning.

  2. A few questions at a time instead of a 6-page form.

  3. "I see you started a Voyage application last week, want to finish it?" — the mid-journey rescue Meena Iyer called "gold."

  4. No re-entry of what was already provided.
