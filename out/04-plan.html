Aurora Bank — Delivery Plan

A proposed path from the prototype to a working system — first two weeks, then 30/60/90.

Discovery draft · illustrative · to validate with Aurora Bank.

First two weeks

Week 1 — assume: scope wire FD-compare to live rates

Point the prototype's FD-Compare-and-Book flow at the real Rates Service so tenure comparisons use current rates instead of sample data.

From you: read access to the Rates Service (sandbox or read-only) and confirmation of its update cadence. assume: sandbox exists

Week 1 — assume: scope ground card recommendations in real terms

Wire Card Recommend-and-Apply to current Aurora Voyage / Everyday / Infinia terms and eligibility criteria, so recommendations reflect what's actually on offer.

From you: current card product terms, and a contact on the card origination system team.

Week 2 — assume: scope & timing prove the authenticated step-up path

Wire NetBanking session pass-through plus OTP step-up for the FD booking flow in a sandbox, so the "compare → recommend → book" hero flow works end to end for a logged-in test account.

From you: sandbox NetBanking login and OTP step-up test credentials, and a point of contact on Vikram's integrations team.

Week 2 — assume: scope & timing wire the mid-journey rescue against one real signal

Surface a real in-progress card application on login, so the assistant can say "want to finish your Voyage application?" against actual data rather than a scripted example.

From you: read access to in-progress application status for a test account. assume: read-only status, not resuming submission yet

Week 2 — assume: scope & timing stand up audit logging

Log every turn that touches customer data — who asked, what was shown, when — so the compliance requirement is provable from day one rather than retrofitted.

From you: agreement on where audit logs should land (e.g. Salesforce or a dedicated log store). assume: destination undecided

30 / 60 / 90 day milestones

  • 30 days assume

    FD Compare-and-Book live for a pilot group of prospective visitors, wired to the real Rates Service, with the OTP step-up flow proven end to end. assume: pilot segment size/definition not yet set

  • 60 days assume

    Card Recommend-and-Apply live alongside FD, with the mid-journey rescue wired against the card origination system, and audit logging running in production for both flows. assume: excludes home/car loans, pending the v1 loan-scope decision

  • 90 days assume

    Basic servicing (balance inquiry, KYC status, application tracking) added for logged-in customers, plus a first pass at personal loan eligibility if greenlit. Card completion-rate and call-deflection metrics tracked against the Recap's targets. assume: depends on the loan-scope and KYC source-of-truth decisions landing first
